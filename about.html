Who we are

Established in 1965 in the Lango sub-region, Atapara Senior Secondary School is a government-aided institution registered under the Ministry of Education and Sports, providing academic, spiritual, and vocational grounding for day and boarding students.

Our work is rooted in disciplined learning, pastoral care, and a commitment to preparing students for meaningful futures in service of their families, communities, and the nation.

Mission

To provide quality secondary education empowering students to become self-reliant and disciplined leaders.

Vision

To be a center of holistic academic excellence and behavioral distinction in Northern Uganda.

School identity

Education

National identity

Uganda National Anthem

The school community upholds the ideals and civic values reflected in the Uganda National Anthem, including unity, patriotism, service, and national responsibility.

Lango regional heritage

Atapara SS values the cultural identity of the Lango sub-region, encouraging respect for local traditions while promoting national citizenship and moral responsibility.
