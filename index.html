School identity

Atapara Senior Secondary School is a government-aided, co-educational institution offering day and boarding education in Aber Sub-county, Oyam District, in the Lango sub-region of Northern Uganda. The school is committed to disciplined learning, strong character, and practical development.

Academic focus

The school supports students through the UNEB framework, providing a balanced education that combines classroom learning, discipline, and practical engagement.

Ordinary Level

Students build a strong foundation in sciences, humanities, languages, computer studies, entrepreneurship, and vocational subjects.

Advanced Level

Senior students pursue science and arts combinations with General Paper, Subsidiary Mathematics, and ICT support.

Holistic development

Learning extends beyond academic work to practical skills, leadership, and values-based personal growth.

Student life

At Atapara SS, student growth includes practical learning, leadership, and faith-based activities. The school encourages involvement in agricultural and vocational work, music, sports, and community responsibilities.

  • Livestock and agricultural projects
  • Young Christian Students and Scripture Union
  • Music, drama, sports, and student leadership

Admissions and contact

Families seeking a place for their children can begin with the official admissions process and contact the school administration for current guidance.

Admission guide

For Senior One, applicants should provide a PLE result slip, completed application form, passport photographs, and a medical report. For Senior Five, applicants should provide a UCE result slip, completed application form, passport photographs, and a medical report.
